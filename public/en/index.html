Model and limits

The installed area minus the deducted area must be positive. The base count is net area ÷ piece area, rounded up. The reserve count is net area × (1 + reserve percentage) ÷ piece area, rounded up. Divide that count by pieces per box and round up again for full boxes. Extra pieces are the purchased count minus the reserve count.

No construction, structural, price, tax or installation-cost advice is provided. Inputs stay in this page; they are not saved, put in a URL or uploaded. Initial loading still downloads website files.

Frequently asked questions

Does this give an exact purchase quantity?

No. It estimates from total area and does not model layout, cuts, or installation loss, so it cannot guarantee the amount you need.

Is the default 10% reserve a recommendation?

No. It is an editable starting assumption, not a universal recommendation.

Do deducted areas need positions?

No. The tool subtracts their summed areas and does not detect overlap or check whether they lie inside an installed area. Avoid subtracting the same space twice.

Can I use it offline?

A page that is already loaded can calculate. Offline reload is not guaranteed because the tool does not provide offline caching.
